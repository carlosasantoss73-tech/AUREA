# Terrazas Costa Limón — Prompt Intelligence Video Test V1

## Purpose
First practical test of AUREA Prompt Intelligence using the Terrazas Costa Limón campaign as a controlled commercial case.

## Source-of-truth boundary
Approved/public facts available in the current commercial governance record: project name, real-estate land project, Esmeraldas/Ecuador, official WhatsApp +593 99 927 6299, official email ventas@terrazascostalimon.com. The formalization packet marks Second Stage, area/lot counts, lot size, prices, financing and specific location details as requiring current documentary approval before public use. Therefore this creative test intentionally avoids unapproved prices, guarantees, inventory counts and legal claims.

## Video A — Ecuador
**Objective:** Generate qualified WhatsApp interest from people in Ecuador who value land, nature, family future and the possibility of learning more about a real-estate project in Esmeraldas.

**Format:** 9:16, premium cinematic, 25–30 seconds.

**Visual direction:** photorealistic coastal Ecuador; natural terrain; warm late-afternoon light; premium real-estate cinematography; restrained luxury; authentic human behavior; no fabricated buildings or infrastructure; no fake maps; no invented amenities.

**Scene plan:**
1. 0–5s: aerial establishing shot of natural coastal terrain and expansive landscape. Text: “Hay lugares que no solo se visitan. Se imaginan como futuro.”
2. 5–11s: close cinematic shots of a couple/family contemplating the landscape, natural and credible. Text: “Terrazas Costa Limón”.
3. 11–18s: controlled aerial/orbit shot showing scale and topography without inventing urbanization. Text: “Esmeraldas, Ecuador”.
4. 18–25s: human/emotional payoff, family walking or observing the land. Text: “Conoce el proyecto. Pregunta. Decide con información.”
5. 25–30s: clean end card: “Solicita información vigente” + WhatsApp +593 99 927 6299.

**Audio:** Spanish Ecuador-neutral voice, warm, confident, conversational, not announcer-like. Natural ambience of coastal wind/birds. Subtle cinematic bed below narration. No exaggerated promises.

**Internal generation prompt:**
Act as a senior cinematic real-estate commercial director. Create a vertical 9:16 photorealistic premium commercial for Terrazas Costa Limón in Esmeraldas, Ecuador. The visual story must feel authentic, aspirational and trustworthy. Show natural coastal terrain and real human emotion, but do not invent buildings, roads, amenities, maps, prices, guarantees, investment returns, legal status or inventory. Use cinematic aerial establishing shots, realistic human movement, physically plausible lighting, natural skin texture, high-end lens rendering and subtle camera motion. Keep all on-screen text minimal and legible. End with a clear WhatsApp call to action: +593 99 927 6299 and “Solicita información vigente”. Spanish narration. 9:16. Premium real-estate advertising. Avoid stock-looking people, plastic skin, excessive saturation, fake architecture, warped geography, unreadable typography, exaggerated luxury and financial promises.

## Video B — Chile / Ecuadorians in Chile
**Objective:** Generate qualified interest from Ecuadorians living in Chile without making nationality-based claims that the ad platform may restrict or that are not necessary for the creative.

**Format:** 9:16, premium cinematic, 25–30 seconds.

**Creative angle:** distance, belonging, family roots and the possibility of reconnecting with Ecuador through a concrete real-estate inquiry. Do not imply legal/financial benefits of cross-border purchase, remote signing, offices, financing or title guarantees unless separately approved.

**Scene plan:**
1. 0–5s: subtle Chilean urban/coastal visual cue, person looking through phone toward Ecuador-related imagery; avoid stereotypes. Text: “A veces, la distancia cambia. Las raíces no.”
2. 5–11s: transition from Chilean environment to authentic-looking Ecuadorian coastal landscape. Text: “Terrazas Costa Limón”.
3. 11–18s: premium aerial/natural terrain sequence. Text: “Esmeraldas, Ecuador”.
4. 18–25s: family/connection moment, phone inquiry visual, no fabricated transaction. Text: “Conoce el proyecto desde donde estés.”
5. 25–30s: end card: “Solicita información vigente” + WhatsApp +593 99 927 6299.

**Audio:** Spanish, neutral Latin American with warm Ecuadorian identity, natural pauses, intimate rather than advertising-heavy. Music should bridge Chile → Ecuador emotionally without using clichéd national stereotypes.

**Internal generation prompt:**
Act as a senior cinematic cross-border real-estate advertising director. Create a vertical 9:16 photorealistic premium commercial aimed at people connected to Ecuador who currently live in Chile. The emotional theme is distance, roots, belonging and curiosity about a real-estate project in Esmeraldas, Ecuador. Use subtle, non-stereotyped Chile-to-Ecuador visual transitions and authentic human behavior. Do not invent offices, remote-signing services, financing, prices, guarantees, legal claims, titles, inventory, infrastructure or investment returns. Use realistic coastal Ecuador terrain, cinematic aerial photography, physically plausible light, premium lens rendering, natural skin and restrained color grading. Keep text minimal and legible. End with “Solicita información vigente” and WhatsApp +593 99 927 6299. Spanish narration, warm conversational delivery, natural ambience and subtle cinematic music. 9:16. Avoid stock-video appearance, artificial faces, plastic skin, warped geography, fake buildings, unreadable text, exaggerated wealth and manipulative urgency.
